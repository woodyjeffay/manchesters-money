What the map shows

Properties in the Manchester City Council area whose ultimate controller, as far as public records show, is outside the UK, plus properties where the ownership chain ends in an entity whose owners are not disclosed (“unknown”).

Registered owner vs ultimate controller

The registered owner is a fact taken from HM Land Registry. The ultimate controller is our reading of a chain of companies and persons with significant control recorded at Companies House and in the Register of Overseas Entities. Each step of the chain is shown with its source so readers can check it.

Sources

  • HM Land Registry: Overseas companies that own property in England and Wales (OCOD)
  • HM Land Registry: UK companies that own property in England and Wales (CCOD)
  • Companies House: company profiles, persons with significant control, Register of Overseas Entities
  • Food Standards Agency: Food Hygiene Rating Scheme
  • postcodes.io (ONS Postcode Directory, OS data)
  • A hand-curated layer of holdings reported in public sources, each with a source link

Limits

  • Companies House does not verify PSC filings; chains can be incomplete or out of date.
  • Where a chain ends in an offshore entity with no disclosed owners, we mark it unknown and do not guess.
  • Individuals are shown only as owners of commercial or investment property, and only where a register names them. Homes held through a company (for example a flat owned by an overseas company) are treated as investment property; the individual is named because the Register of Overseas Entities or the PSC register names them.
